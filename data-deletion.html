Data Deletion Policy

How XOMA handles your data deletion requests

🔒 Your Data Rights: You have complete control over your personal data. This policy explains how we handle data deletion requests and what happens to your information.

📋 Types of Data We Store

👤 Personal Information

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Profile picture and bio
  • Account preferences and settings

🛍️ Marketplace Data

  • Item listings (books, uniforms, stationery)
  • Transaction history and payment records
  • Messages and communications
  • Reviews and ratings

📊 Usage Analytics

  • App usage patterns (anonymized)
  • Device information and preferences
  • Location data (when shared)

🗑️ Data Deletion Options

Deletion Type What's Deleted Timeline Reversible
Specific Data Individual items, messages, or records Immediate No
Account Data All personal information and content 7 days No
Complete Deletion Everything including backups 30 days No

📱 How to Request Data Deletion

Method 1: In-App Deletion

  1. Open XOMA app → Profile → Settings
  2. Go to "Privacy & Security"
  3. Select "Manage My Data"
  4. Choose what to delete
  5. Confirm your request

Method 2: Email Request

Send to: alvinondieki5@gmail.com

Subject: Data Deletion Request

Include:

  • Your registered email address
  • Specific data you want deleted
  • Reason for deletion (optional)

⏰ Deletion Timeline

📅 What Happens When

  • 0-24 hours: Request received and verified
  • 1-3 days: Data removed from active systems
  • 7 days: Data purged from primary databases
  • 30 days: Complete removal from all backups

🔄 What We Cannot Delete

Some data may be retained for legal or security reasons:

  • Legal Requirements: Data required by law (tax records, etc.)
  • Security Logs: Anonymized security and fraud prevention data
  • Aggregated Analytics: Anonymous usage statistics
  • Active Disputes: Data related to ongoing legal matters
📝 Note: Even when we cannot delete certain data, it is anonymized and cannot be linked back to you personally.

✅ Verification Process

To protect your privacy, we verify all deletion requests:

  1. Identity Verification: Confirm you own the account
  2. Request Review: Ensure the request is legitimate
  3. Confirmation: Send confirmation before deletion
  4. Completion Notice: Notify when deletion is complete

🔍 Data Export Before Deletion

Before deleting your data, you can request a copy:

  • Personal Information: Profile and account details
  • Content: Your listings and messages
  • Activity: Transaction and usage history
  • Format: JSON or CSV files
  • Delivery: Secure download link via email
