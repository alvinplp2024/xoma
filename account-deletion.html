Delete Your XOMA Account

Permanently remove your account and all associated data

⚠️ Important Notice: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Please read this page carefully before proceeding.

🗑️ What Gets Deleted

When you delete your XOMA account, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Profile Information: Name, email, phone number, and profile picture
  • Listings: All your book, uniform, and stationery listings
  • Messages: All conversations and chat history
  • Transaction History: Purchase and sales records
  • Credits: Any remaining credits in your account
  • Reviews & Ratings: All reviews you've given or received
  • Preferences: App settings and notification preferences
🚨 Cannot Be Recovered: Once deleted, your account and data cannot be restored. Make sure to download any important information before proceeding.

📱 How to Delete Your Account

Method 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)

  1. Open the XOMA app
  2. Go to Profile → Settings
  3. Scroll down to Privacy & Security
  4. Tap "Delete Account"
  5. Follow the confirmation steps
  6. Enter your password to confirm

Method 2: Email Request

If you cannot access the app, send an email to our support team:

  • Subject: Account Deletion Request
  • Include: Your registered email address
  • Include: Reason for deletion (optional)
  • Response Time: 3-5 business days

⏰ Deletion Timeline

  • Immediate: Account access is disabled
  • 24 hours: Profile and listings are hidden from other users
  • 7 days: All personal data is permanently deleted from our servers
  • 30 days: Backup data is purged from all systems
📋 Before You Delete:
  • Complete any pending transactions
  • Download your transaction history if needed
  • Use any remaining credits
  • Save important messages or contact information

🔄 Alternatives to Deletion

Consider these options instead of permanent deletion:

  • Deactivate Account: Temporarily disable your account
  • Privacy Settings: Adjust what information is visible
  • Notification Settings: Turn off unwanted notifications
  • Data Export: Download your data without deleting
