App Review Support

Apple App Store review can use the credentials provided in the App Review Information section of App Store Connect.

  • Use the dedicated reviewer account configured in App Store Connect.
  • If login fails, provide additional access details by replying to the reviewer notes email.
  • Mention any required environment setup before deep testing.
